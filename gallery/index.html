How it works

Found, not generated.

Accidental Haiku finds sequences of existing words that can be read in a 5–7–5 syllable structure. It never invents or rearranges the words.

Readers may eventually submit a discovery for consideration, but that requires an explicit action. Before anything appears here, a human checks accuracy, privacy and publication rights.
