Submission guidelines

Submissions are not open yet. This is a preview of the rules. The gallery does not currently collect or receive poems, email addresses, or browsing data.

What may be submitted?

Only a three-line 5–7–5 passage using exactly the source's original consecutive words, discovered on a genuinely public webpage. Never submit content from private messages, email, accounts requiring sign-in, medical or financial records, workplaces, or other confidential material.

Permission and attribution

The person who discovers a haiku may not own the underlying words. A public webpage and a credit line do not automatically grant republication rights. We plan to prioritize public-domain, permissively licensed, or otherwise authorized material. A moderator must check rights before publication.

How publication will work

Future submissions will require an explicit preview and confirmation, separate from ordinary copying or sharing. Every entry will be reviewed before being displayed publicly. The collection will not collect browsing history automatically.

Privacy and removal

We plan to publish the selected poem, its public source link and necessary attribution only. Data collection, hosting provider, retention periods, the contact for removal requests, and the lawful basis for processing will be documented in a revised privacy notice before submissions open. No collection is enabled by this preview.
