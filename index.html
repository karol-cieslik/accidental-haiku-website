Accidental Haiku privacy policy

Publisher: Karol Cieślik. Applies to Accidental Haiku 1.0.2. Updated 24 November 2026.

What stays in your browser

The extension reads visible prose on supported pages to find passages with a 5–7–5 syllable pattern. This can include visible articles, posts, comments or messages. It checks the current page address to recognize supported pages and navigation. Page text, detected poems and their positions are processed locally in tab memory. Input fields, forms and editable regions are skipped.

Storage and sharing

The extension stores three preferences in Chrome’s local extension storage: whether it is enabled, its scanning mode and its display mode. It has no account, backend or analytics. The developer receives no webpage content, URLs, poems or usage logs from the extension and does not sell or share those data. Copy places only the selected poem on your clipboard when you ask. Share opens your device’s share sheet or copies the poem with its source link and extension credit. Query parameters and fragments are removed. You choose the recipient or where to paste. Save image creates a poetry card on your device.

Your controls

Manual scanning is the default. Automatic scanning requires additional site access, which you can revoke in Chrome’s extension settings. Turning the extension off clears its displayed results and stops scanning; closing a tab releases its in-memory results. Removing the extension clears its saved preferences. Clipboard contents are managed by your operating system.

For questions, use the developer contact in the Chrome Web Store listing.
